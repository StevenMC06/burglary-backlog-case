Architecture

AI proposes. The engine verifies. A detective decides.

Connectors

MCP server (Rust): records, pawn feed, verify_series, solvability, citation checker, approval queue, cost model

Agents

Extractor (Haiku) · Triage (Sonnet) · Series Hunter (Sonnet) · Skeptic (Opus) · Packet Writer (Sonnet) · Victim Liaison (Haiku)

Teams

Intake & Triage (per report) · Series Hunting (hunter ⇄ skeptic ⇄ engine, nightly) · Victim Comms (on status change)

Channels

Sergeant console · detective packet · victim message draft (any language, staff-approved). Not built: patrol hands-free brief.

Approvals never block: requests queue with a ticket and escalate after 120 min, so human review waits hours, not days.

Clearance logic

How 13% becomes 15.6%: new leads, worked by detectives

Step (Base)Cases/yr
Open cases linked to an arrest (1,300 cleared × 30% hit × 1.5 per hit × 85% adoption)497
Pawn/serial hits (10,000 × 35% traceable × 10% resurface × 85% × (1 − 20% already linked))238
Moderate cases newly worked (120 × 85% adoption)102
New leads → 65% completed → 50% clear, minus 10 lost to screening837 → +262
Base gives 15.6%, not the 16.25% target (+325). Only Optimistic (+458) clears it. The 50% conversion is unbenchmarked (today's clearance is 13%); at the conservative 35% the result is +45. Every factor is an assumption the pilot measures.

Follow-up hours are a cost, not a saving. False links cost detective time (184 false leads dismissed at 20 min each, 80% precision assumed; our eval measures 66% pair precision on synthetic data, so the real number is a pilot question).

Pilot

Prove it on one precinct before scaling

  • Days 0–30: integrate the RMS export and pawn feed in a CJIS-approved environment; train 1 sergeant and 6 detectives; replay 6 months of closed cases to measure link precision.
  • Days 31–90: randomized pilot: half of new burglaries get Casefile packets, half business as usual.
  • Months 4–12: expand only if the gates pass; quarterly accuracy and equity audits.
The one metric: cleared burglaries per detective-hour, pilot vs. control. It captures both asks, savings and clearance, at once.

Gates: link precision on replay meets the bar we set with the department; zero un-reviewed actions; no drop in clearance on screened cases.

Ethics, trust and the ask

Cases, not people. Humans decide. Everything is auditable.

  • Bias: links cases, never scores people or neighbourhoods; no predictive patrol; equity monitor on screening rates.
  • Privacy: CJIS-approved hosting; only data the department lawfully holds; no facial recognition.
  • False positives: skeptic agent plus rules engine rejected 10/10 decoy series; a serial match is a lead, not ownership.
  • Human in the loop: no send, assign or alert action exists without a click; hash-chained audit log; replays give identical output.
