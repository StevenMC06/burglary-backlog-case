# Economics and clearance model

## Cost formulas
Annual gross labor value = eligible cases × adoption rate × net minutes saved per case / 60 × role hourly cost.
Net minutes saved includes human review, corrections, and added workflow steps.
Year-one net benefit = gross labor value − build/integration/training costs − operating costs − added follow-up labor.
Steady-state net benefit excludes one-time build costs but includes recurring support and refresh costs.
Year-one ROI = (year-one benefits − year-one costs) / year-one costs.
Payback requires a rollout-aware cash-flow schedule; do not assume full adoption on day one.

Report cash savings separately from capacity redeployed to investigations. Do not count the same hours as both budget savings and free additional detective work.

## Illustrative hurdle, not forecast
90 officer minutes saved × 10,000 cases / 60 × $50 = $750,000 gross annual labor value.
This assumes 100% eligible coverage and adoption. To reach $457,500 net, all included annual costs must total no more than $292,500. First-year ramp-up can materially reduce benefits.

## Clearance
Target = 1,625 cleared cases, versus baseline 1,300.
Incremental clearances = additional verified actionable leads × follow-up completion × incremental clearance conversion, adjusted for overlap with cases that would already clear.
All conversion assumptions remain unproven. Model conservative/base/optimistic scenarios. Faster review alone does not establish clearance improvement.
