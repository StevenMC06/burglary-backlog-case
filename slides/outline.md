# Eight-slide working outline

1. Hook and recommendation: the chosen bottleneck, decision requested, and realistic outcome.
2. Baseline: where time and money go; why this bottleneck wins over alternatives.
3. Workflow: user, inputs, AI assistance, source verification, human decision.
4. Working demo: 60–90 second recording with synthetic data; show a failure/correction.
5. Economics: assumptions, eligible volume, adoption, net time, build/run costs, ROI/payback, sensitivity.
6. Clearance mechanism: explain the path to 325 additional clearances and what is unproven.
7. Pilot: first 90 days and year one; controlled comparison, training, primary metric, rollout gate.
8. Ethics and decision: bias, privacy, false positives, over-policing, audit trail, human authority.

Total presentation budget: seven minutes, including demo. Reserve three minutes for Q&A. Final slides live in the shared deck linked from README.
